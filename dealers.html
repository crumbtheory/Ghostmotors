What about fees?

Direct dealer trades avoid the high fees associated with traditional auctions. Ghost Motors will discuss any applicable service fees or transaction terms with you before you commit to a deal.

What happens after I apply?

We review your dealership and buyer details, learn what inventory you want to buy or sell, and follow up about relevant opportunities. Availability and lead volume vary.

Questions

Know where you stand.

Who sets the price?

You set your asking price or buying criteria, choose which opportunities to pursue, and decide whether to accept a deal.

Do I have to use an auction?

No. Ghost Motors is built around direct connections between dealers. The parties discuss and agree on the transaction terms.

Are leads or sales guaranteed?

No. We work to bring relevant opportunities to the network, but the number of leads, availability, and results vary.

Is GhostIQ available now?

Not yet. GhostIQ is in development. You can apply to the existing dealer network using the form above.

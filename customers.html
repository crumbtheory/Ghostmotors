Questions

Good to know before you submit.

Do I need to contact dealers myself?

No. Ghost Motors shops your vehicle through its network and brings available options back to you. A dealer may need to speak with you later if you decide to move forward.

What if my car has a loan?

The form asks about your loan status. Share that information so we can account for it when reviewing your vehicle and discussing next steps.

Is an offer guaranteed in 30 minutes?

No. Thirty minutes is a target, and timing can vary depending on the information provided and dealer response.

Am I required to accept an offer?

Submitting information lets us seek options for you. You decide whether to proceed with a transaction.

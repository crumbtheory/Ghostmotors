What to expect when you use ghostmotors.co or submit a request through our linked forms.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

About the service

Ghost Motors connects vehicle sellers and dealerships. We may review customer vehicle information, seek dealer interest, identify inventory opportunities, and introduce dealers who want to discuss a direct trade. The website describes the service and provides links to customer and dealer intake forms.

Submitting information

Provide information you believe is accurate and current, including vehicle condition, ownership, loan status, photos, contact details, and dealer information where relevant. We may ask for more information before presenting a vehicle or opportunity to another party.

Submitting a form does not require Ghost Motors or any dealer to make an offer. It does not by itself create a purchase, sale, or financing agreement.

Offers and timing

Any offer depends on the vehicle, the accuracy of the information provided, dealer interest, and any further review or inspection. An indicated turnaround time is a target, not a guarantee. You decide whether to accept an available option; a transaction moves forward only on terms agreed by the parties involved.

Dealer opportunities and fees

Dealers can choose which leads or inventory opportunities to pursue and can negotiate transaction terms directly. Ghost Motors promotes direct connections that avoid the high fees associated with traditional auctions. If a Ghost Motors service fee or other charge applies to a particular opportunity, its terms will be discussed before a party commits to that transaction. No fee schedule is established by this website.

Third-party services

The customer and dealer forms are hosted by Google Forms. When you use those forms, Google's terms and privacy practices also apply. Links to other websites or services are provided for convenience; their content and practices are controlled by their operators.

Website content

Information on this site may change as our network and services develop. GhostIQ is described as a platform in development and is not presented as an available service. Contact us if you need current details about the network, a specific opportunity, or applicable transaction terms.

Privacy and contact

Read our Privacy Notice to learn how submitted information may be used and shared. Questions about these terms or a potential transaction can be sent to ghostmotors.co@gmail.com.

We shop dealer offers for sellers and connect dealerships with leads, inventory, and direct trading opportunities.

FAQs

Short answers.
Clear next steps.

Questions from both sides of the transaction, without the fine-print overload.

Customers

How quickly can I get an offer?

Our goal is to provide a competitive offer within 30 minutes after we receive the information needed to evaluate your vehicle.

Can you help if I still have a loan?

Yes. Ghost Motors can work with vehicles that still have a payoff balance and help structure the transaction around the existing loan.

Do I need to contact dealers myself?

No. Give us your vehicle details once. We shop it across our dealer network, compare interest and offers, and bring the options to you. You choose whether to move forward.

What vehicles do you buy?

We evaluate a broad range of vehicles. Final eligibility and pricing depend on condition, history, market demand, and payoff details.

Dealers

How does dealer-to-dealer sourcing work?

Tell us what you want to buy, sell, or trade. We highlight relevant inventory and introduce interested dealers directly. You control pricing, which leads to pursue, and the final decision.

How large is the network?

Ghost Motors currently works across a network of 237 dealers.

Are there auction fees?

Dealer-to-dealer opportunities are negotiated directly, so you can avoid the high fees that typically come with an auction.

What is GhostIQ?

GhostIQ is our upcoming B2B dealer software focused on AI-assisted sourcing, pricing intelligence, inventory recommendations, and workflow automation.

Is GhostIQ available now?

Not yet. The platform is in development and will be introduced to dealers in stages.
